# Changelog

All notable changes to KazBars will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Target inspect panel** (Extras ▸ Inspect panel…) — an optional in-game overlay that shows a combat sheet for whatever you have targeted, styled after the game's own inspect window: armor and protection with their mitigation percentages, crit and critigation, heal rating with the Celestial Gaze range it buys, bonus spell damage, combat rating — and, on player targets only, the PvP section with its own bonus spell damage and combat rating (mobs and bosses show the PvE sheet). The title line identifies the target — name, class, and level/PvP level, dropping whatever a target doesn't have. A Sections group in the dialog offers two toggles, both on by default and applied on your next Build & Install: **Show the PvP section** governs the PvP block (on by default, so nothing changes unless you untick it), and **Track slotted perks** adds a Perks row at the bottom of the panel on player targets — up to six of the game's own buff icons for the AA perks detected on the target. The row mirrors the game's own perk bar — six boxes in three colour-coded pairs, two blue for General, two red for Archetype, two grey for Class — so a perk always appears in its own pair and the colour tells you what kind it is. Three of every class's seven perks take both class slots, and the panel paints those across both Class boxes just as the game does, so they read as two identical icons rather than a gap. A box is empty when the target hasn't filled that slot, or when an active perk isn't currently running — only passive perks and active ones that are running show. A perk you were given by a groupmate can show as well, and empty slots draw as empty boxes. Turn either off and the built overlay drops that section entirely. Turn it on in the new dialog and it ships with your next Build & Install; the position and font size you pick are baked in, and the whole panel scales with the font size. Dragging its name strip shows live coordinates — type them into the dialog to make a spot permanent; the − button folds the panel down to that strip, and Aoc.exe clients remember both the drag and the folded state. Off by default; when off, the built overlay carries no inspect-panel code at all.
- **Seal of Yog (Crit) and Seal of Yog (Mana)** — the Dark Templar stacking buffs join the catalog, every stack rank covered. (Already delivered to existing installs as a content update; see `database-changelog.md`.)
- **Every combat number now gets a direction, not just a color** (Extras ▸ Damage number colors…). Each of the ~35 sources gains a **Rising** / **Dropping** / **Zig-zag** dropdown beside its swatch — float it above the head, drop it into the fixed column, or add it to the zig-zag stack — named to match the Damage Numbers panel's position cards. A **Number directions** card at the top carries two shortcuts that move a whole group at once: **Group my resource numbers** sends your own mana and stamina losses down to the fixed column where your gains already land, and **Send incoming numbers to the fixed column** does the same for everything that hits you. Both simply set the per-source dropdowns, so you can fine-tune any row afterwards — the checkbox unticks once its group is mixed. "Reset to game default" now restores the stock colour *and* direction, per row or for the whole panel.
- **Text size for the in-game stopwatch** (Extras ▸ Stopwatch…). An 8–48 font size, baked in at build time, that scales the whole panel — collapsed bar included, so it matches the inspect panel set to the same size.

### Changed
- **Damage number colors now apply the moment you hit Apply — no build, no toggle.** The color editor (Extras ▸ Damage number colors…) works like the Default Buff Bars editor: pick your colors, click Apply, and type `/reloadui` in-game to see them. It edits the game's `TextColors.xml` directly instead of waiting for a Build & Install, and it no longer depends on the Damage Numbers mod being switched on — colors are the game's own feature, so they stand on their own. Edits land in your `Customized` skin folder (created for you if needed) and touch only what you set, so a game update can't wipe them. "Reset to game default" pulls the original color straight from the game's stock files. Colors you set now stay put when you turn the Damage Numbers mod off or uninstall — use Reset if you want them back to stock.
- **Your number directions are yours now.** The Damage Numbers mod, Build & Install and Uninstall no longer touch `TextColors.xml` at all — colours and directions both belong to the Damage number colors editor, which writes them the moment you hit Apply. Turning the mod off, rebuilding, or uninstalling no longer reverts a direction you chose; "Reset to game default" is how you get stock back, exactly as it already worked for colours.
- **Two Damage Numbers toggles now say what they actually do.** "Separate resources into Column B" is now **Split signed numbers into Column B**: of the numbers already dropping into the fixed column, plain damage stays in Column A and the signed ones — heals, stamina, mana — move to Column B. "Group my resource numbers" is now **Keep enemy drains overhead**: mana and stamina you drain from enemies keep floating over them even when your own resource numbers are set to Dropping. Both are settings baked into the mod; *which* sources drop into the fixed column is now picked per source in Damage number colors, where the "Group my resource numbers" shortcut moved.
- **The Damage Numbers panel waits for Apply.** Settings no longer save as you drag a slider: **Apply** saves and closes, and Cancel, Escape or the X discard everything you changed. Loading a preset stages it the same way.
- **The Extras menu is split into two labelled groups** — the editors that write your game files (Default buff bars, Damage number colors), applied with `/reloadui`, and the ones that ship with your next Build & Install (Damage number mod, Stopwatch, Inspect panel, Cast timer). "In-game stopwatch…" is now **Stopwatch…** and "Target inspect panel…" is now **Inspect panel…**. Both dialogs now carry those names in their title bar and header too, and the in-app guide follows.
- **The Cast Timer moved out of the Grids panel into its own dialog** (Extras ▸ Cast timer…) — and its settings are now saved on your PC instead of in your profile, so **anyone who already had a cast timer set has to open the new dialog and set it once more**. The strip that used to sit pinned above the grid list is gone; everything it carried is in the dialog, with the same Apply flow as the Stopwatch and Inspect panel: one **Include the cast timer in builds** master toggle, Player position and Target position with their X/Y, and a Text section for Bold, font size (8–48), what the timer shows (Elapsed / Total / Both) and its colour, with the live sample preview beside them. Apply saves and closes; Cancel, Escape and the X discard. Nothing reaches the game until your next Build & Install, exactly as before. The settings sit with the stopwatch and the inspect panel in your app preferences because the positions depend on your screen, not on your buff layout — so switching profiles leaves the timer alone, and a profile someone shares with you no longer brings the sender's cast-timer positions with it. Your old profiles still contain the cast-timer block: nothing reads it, loading one neither applies it nor errors, and it drops out the next time you save that profile.
- **The buff-discovery console toggle moved into the Inspect panel dialog** (Extras ▸ Inspect panel… ▸ Console), saved with that dialog's Apply. It's the other in-game inspection tool, so both switch on from one place.
- **The in-game stopwatch and buff console match the inspect panel.** Both were re-skinned to the same warm near-black plate, bronze frame, Conan-orange title and square corners, with the stopwatch's time reading green, orange or grey by state. The collapsed stopwatch bar can be clicked to open the panel again, and the console remembers where you dragged it and can't be pushed off screen. The stopwatch and inspect dialogs also reopen where you left them, and Escape closes them.
- **The buff-discovery console collapses like the rest of the overlay.** A − button on its title line folds the console down to a small labelled bar — the same bar the stopwatch and inspect panel collapse to — and clicking that bar opens it again. Whether you left it open or collapsed is remembered along with where you dragged it: Aoc.exe clients restore both across a relog, other clients start it open in the middle of the screen.
- **The reset button in Damage number colors sits at the end of its row.** Each source now reads label, direction, swatch, then ↺ — the reset comes after both of the things it resets instead of sitting between them.

### Fixed
- **Tooltips no longer get stuck on screen.** Removing a control while its tooltip was showing — deleting a grid card, for example — could leave the tooltip floating over the app. Tooltips now disappear with their control.
- **Buff console entries read in the console's own type again.** Every logged buff name and ID was falling back to a mismatched serif font; entries now render in the same face as the rest of the panel — the buff name in parchment grey, `ID: nnnn` in green.

## [2.2.2] — 2026-06-17

### Changed
- **The build screen reads as clear steps instead of a flicker.** Now that the build is near-instant, its progress used to flash past too fast to follow. The compile and install run without freezing the loading animation, and each phase — Compiling, Baking damage numbers, Installing — holds on screen for a brief beat, so you can see what's happening.

### Fixed
- **Build & Install no longer freezes for ~15 seconds before it starts.** On some systems, clicking Build & Install would hang with nothing on screen for a beat while Windows prepared the helper processes the build needs (compiling the grids and checking whether the game is running). That preparation step now runs without the stall, so Build & Install starts near-instantly — roughly 16 seconds down to under one on affected machines.

## [2.2.1] — 2026-06-16

### Fixed
- **Frameless popups no longer trap the app after Show Desktop (Win+D).** Pressing Win+D while a borderless KazBars modal was open — the Build & Install summary, the welcome popup, About, or the "close the game first" notice — could leave the window minimized with no way back: clicking the taskbar or Alt-Tab did nothing, because these title-bar-less windows give Windows nothing to restore. They now track the main window — hiding when you minimize and returning, on top and usable, when you restore.
- **Veil of the Unliving (Zaal) now matches the in-game effect.** Corrected the buff's spell ID, so grids and profiles tracking it light up again. (Already delivered to existing installs as a content update; see `database-changelog.md`.)

## [2.2.0] — 2026-06-15

### Added
- **Damage Numbers** (Game ▸ Damage number Mod… / Damage number Colors…) — a new panel that tunes Age of Conan's floating combat-number overlay and installs a leaner, faster rewrite of it on your next Build & Install. The headline fix: damage numbers no longer shrink to nothing at range — a **Keep ranged numbers big** toggle holds the size of *ranged* hits (past ~15 real metres) without ever touching your close-up (melee) numbers, which stay exactly as the game draws them. Also tune shadow style (None / Fast / Real), pop-in and fade speed, and where numbers land (above the target, in fixed columns, or a zig-zag stack), with Default / Performance presets. A **Group my resource numbers** toggle routes your own mana/stamina losses into the same fixed column as your resource gains — so all your resource changes read in one place — while mana/stamina you drain from enemies still floats over them. A **Separate resources into Column B** toggle drops everything that lands on you (incoming damage, heals, mana/stamina) into fixed columns and splits it — damage in one column; heals, stamina and mana in the next — for a clean, stationary readout instead of numbers flying off your head. And a **Damage number colors** editor lets you recolor every combat-number source independently — incoming vs outgoing hits, crits, spells, combos, heals, mana/stamina — laid out self on the left, your target on the right. Off by default behind a single master toggle; your stock `DamageInfo.swf` is backed up once, so turning it off or uninstalling restores the original.
- **In-game stopwatch** (Game ▸ In-game stopwatch…) — an optional Start / Pause / Reset count-up timer that lives *inside* the game as part of the KazBars overlay, so it works in fullscreen and its buttons never steal focus from the game. Turn it on in the new dialog and it ships with your next Build & Install: a compact draggable panel showing `h:mm:ss`, with a − button that collapses it to just its title bar (which then shows the running time). Dragging the title bar shows live coordinates — type them into the dialog to make a spot permanent; Aoc.exe clients remember the position and collapsed state automatically. Off by default; when off, the built overlay carries no stopwatch code at all.

### Changed
- **Deeps "Alarm & Tints" is simpler to set.** The DPS-out alarm is now a slider over a 1000–4000/s band (instead of a typed value), and the four ΔHP-in survival-tint thresholds collapse into two presets — **Standard** (DPS / healers) and **Tank** (a wider symmetric band) — with a caption that restates the breakpoints. The default survival tints changed to match the **Standard** preset; the alarm default stays 2500/s. Your saved alarm value is kept and clamped into the slider's band.
- **Buff catalog reorganized.** The shipped buff database is sorted into clearer categories — a new **#Protections** group for group damage-mitigation buffs, plus plainer names (#Resistances → #Immunities, #Global → #General, #Group HoT → #Group Heals) and the raid tiers grouped under **#Raid T3…T6**. Display grouping only — no spell IDs changed, so existing grids and profiles are unaffected. (Shipped as a content update; see `database-changelog.md`.)
- **Refreshed default profile.** The out-of-the-box grids were redesigned and renamed for clarity — **My Buffs**, **Raid Debuffs**, **Target Buffs**, **Target Debuffs** — authored against a 1440p reference that scales to any resolution.
- **Internal** (no user-facing change) — `ui_widgets.py` split into focused modules: `ui_headers.py` (dialog/app headers), `ui_forms.py` (form fields + shared settings-panel builders), and `ui_collapsible.py` (`CollapsibleSection`), leaving `ui_widgets` as the leaf core (tooltips, toasts, event bindings).

### Fixed
- **Cast Timer no longer flashes a huge bogus estimate during lag or cast interrupts.** The timer-only overlay now samples the clock and cast progress on the same engine frame instead of on a free-running interval, so a stutter can't pair a fresh clock with stale progress and spike the estimate.
